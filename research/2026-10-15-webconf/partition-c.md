## C01. Fitting Into Any Shape: A Flexible LLM-Based Re-Ranker With Configurable Depth and Width

**Read: F, related author preprint titled Matryoshka Re-Ranker: A Flexible Re-Ranking Architecture With Configurable Depth and Width.** [Certain] WWW 2025 title/DOI: [10.1145/3696410.3714620](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714620); [full preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.16302v1). The architecture exposes intermediate scoring heads and attention-weighted sequence compression; cascaded self-distillation and factorized LoRA compensation preserve compressed configurations. Evaluation uses MS MARCO passage/document ranking, TREC DL19/20, and 14 public BEIR datasets, mostly reranking top-100 BGE candidates. [Certain] §3.1.4 instructs users to choose compression using validation precision; no dedicated limitations section was found. [Likely] A global quality/compute frontier does not establish per-query ranking preservation: different documents can change scores unequally, crossing rank boundaries. **Extension, medium feasibility:** evaluate loss of relevant top-k documents under adaptive depth/width, calibrate escalation from score-margin trajectories, and compare against fixed compression, budget-matched random routing, and full inference. An early-exit mechanism alone is not novel; later systems already adapt early exits.

## C02. GraphCLIP: Enhancing Transferability in Graph Foundation Models for Text-Attributed Graphs

**Read: F.** [Certain] [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10329v3), [author implementation/checkpoints](https://github.com/ZhuYun97/GraphCLIP). It constructs graph-summary pairs with Qwen2-72B, aligns a graph transformer with frozen text representations using invariant contrastive learning, and adds graph prompt tuning. §4 uses five source TAGs and seven target graphs across academic, e-commerce, social, and Wikipedia domains; tasks include zero-shot node classification/link prediction and few-shot adaptation. [Certain] Appendix H explicitly says complex edge attributes are not incorporated. Appendix G reports eight A100-40GB GPUs for seven-hour pretraining. [Likely] Graph-summary alignment can transmit summary omissions or unsupported details; prediction transfer does not establish semantic faithfulness of the supervision. **Extension, medium feasibility:** use released checkpoints and controlled graph/text interventions to compare topology-sensitive versus text-only transfer and quantify summary-induced shortcut dependence. Reproducing full summary generation/pretraining within the stated resources is substantially less feasible than checkpoint evaluation.

## C03. Sherlock: Towards Multi-scene Video Abnormal Event Extraction and Localization via a Global-local Spatial-sensitive LLM

**Read: F.** [Certain] [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.18863v1), [publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714617). Sherlock builds spatial experts and a balancing loss over Video-LLaVA for subject/type/object/scene quadruples plus temporal localization. CUVA supplies surveillance videos, QA-derived annotations, and timestamps; ChatGPT proposes quadruples and humans constrain/check elements. Evaluation includes exact element/quadruple F1, GPT/T5 scores, temporal mAP, and anomaly classification. [Certain] §6 proposes event relationships, inference, and explanatory outputs. §4.4 reports **16 h stage 1 + 60 h stage 2 + 8 h inference on A100-40GB**. [Likely] Shared event/entity vocabularies and original CUVA splits leave open generalization to new camera/event combinations; this does not show failure. **Extension, low feasibility for full reproduction:** compositional event/scenario holdouts using a released checkpoint; medium only for a small frozen-model audit. Sequential reported training already exceeds 72 h on the authors' hardware.

## C04. InfoMAE: Pair-Efficient Cross-Modal Alignment for Multimodal Time-Series Sensing Signals

**Read: F.** [Certain] [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.09707v1), [WWW DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714853). Independent unimodal pretraining is followed by shared/private information alignment, masked reconstruction, augmentation consistency, and temporal locality constraints. Experiments use seismic/acoustic Moving Object Detection from three domains and HAR from RW-HAR/PAMAP2; evaluate downstream accuracy/F1, linear probing, modality combinations, and paired-data fractions, including 1%-3% pairs in Appendix E. [Certain] Appendix F explicitly acknowledges autoencoder/discriminator training overhead and residual sampling bias under sparse pairing. Appendix D reports pretraining up to two days on RTX4090-24GB. [Likely] Randomly reducing synchronized pairs need not model practical missingness depending on activity, device failure, or domain. **Extension, medium feasibility with checkpoints:** fixed-budget pair acquisition under missing-not-at-random mechanisms, with random, diversity, and uncertainty acquisition baselines and untouched participant/domain tests. Full multi-dataset pretraining plus selection development is risky in three days.

## C05. LargePiG for Hallucination-Free Query Generation: Your Large Language Model is Secretly a Pointer Generator

**Read: F, preprint uses shorter LargePiG title.** [Certain] [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.11366v1), [author code and final-title declaration](https://github.com/Jeryi-Sun/LargePiG). Training-free decoding mixes vocabulary probabilities with source-token attention; inter-layer distribution divergence controls copying. TruthfulVQG is Chinese video-derived and TruthfulDQG English document-derived; evaluation includes reference-query MC1/MC2/MC3 probabilities, human generation assessment, FACTOR, and multimodal extensions. [Certain] The closing discussion states that experiments mainly use 7B models and suggests better layer selection; the precise heading was not independently pinned. [Likely] Copying source vocabulary cannot by itself preserve relations, polarity, attribution, or source reliability. **Extension, medium feasibility:** source-controlled entity/polarity/attribution minimal pairs; measure free-generated query entailment and downstream retrieval, not only reference likelihood. Compare normal decoding, LargePiG, constrained extraction, and generate-then-verify. This needs local attention/logit access; API key count does not supply these internals.

## C06. A Cooperative Multi-Agent Framework for Zero-Shot Named Entity Recognition

**Read: F.** [Certain] [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.18702v1), [WWW DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714923), [author code](https://github.com/WZH-NLP/WWW25-CMAS). CMAS coordinates self-annotation, type-related feature extraction, demonstration discrimination, and prediction. CoNLL03, WikiGold, WNUT17, OntoNotes, GENIA, and BioNLP11 support exact-span/type micro-F1; the paper samples test examples three times to control API expense and evaluates GPT, Llama, and Qwen backbones. [Certain] §5 restricts type-related text features to 1-grams; no dedicated limitations section was found. [Likely] Agents sharing a backbone and pseudo-labeled demonstrations can propagate common errors; multiple roles are not evidence of statistically independent information. **Extension, high computational feasibility:** isolate whether discriminator gains persist under matched token/call budgets, corrupted pseudo labels, and type-boundary ambiguities, comparing self-consistency and a single-agent structured prompt. This is a controlled ablation opportunity, not established novelty for another generic multi-agent framework.

## C07. Path-LLM: A Multi-Modal Path Representation Learning by Aligning and Fusing with Large Language Models

**Read: P, methods via indexed anonymous manuscript plus accepted institutional metadata.** [Certain] [Accepted publication record](https://vbn.aau.dk/en/publications/path-llm-a-multi-modal-path-representation-learning-by-aligning-a/), [WWW DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714744), [author code](https://github.com/decisionintelligence/Path-LLM), [older manuscript](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=KmMSQS6tFn). It aligns road topology and OpenStreetMap text through TPalign, gated TPfusion, frozen GPT2 blocks with selected adaptations, and overlapping curriculum learning. [Certain] Accepted metadata reports three real-world datasets and path representation downstream tasks; the older anonymous abstract reports two. Dataset/task-level evaluation details were not fully verified. Do not confuse it with unrelated shortest-path graph Path-LLM arXiv:2408.05456. **Author limitations: unavailable for confirmation.** [Likely] Road names may encode geographic identity rather than transferable structure. **Extension, medium conditional feasibility:** rename-place, missing-text, and cross-city topology-preserving tests against topology-only and text-only encoders. Fulltext/version and data-contract verification must precede execution.

## C08. Explainable Multi-Modality Alignment for Transferable Recommendation

**Read: F, author-hosted PDF pp.5-8 inspected.** [Certain] [Author paper/repository](https://github.com/ysh-1998/EARec), [direct PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ysh-1998/EARec/main/blob/The_WebConf_2025_.pdf), [WWW DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714733). EARec separately aligns textual, visual, and behavior modalities to item titles, merges modality adapters, then freezes item encoders for downstream sequence recommendation. Five Amazon source domains transfer to Office/Arts/Instruments and MovieLens, with leave-one-out HR/NDCG over the full item catalog. [Certain] §4.4, pp.7-8, evaluates explainability through generated-title RougeL and its relationship to recommendation; it speculates limited behavior data can cause overfitting. No dedicated limitations section was located. [Likely] Recovering an anchor title does not establish faithful modality attribution or preserved recommendation-relevant distinctions. **Extension, medium feasibility:** fixed-embedding modality/anchor interventions and counterfactual attribute controls, testing whether explanation similarity predicts actual recommendation sensitivity. Released modality LoRA weights reduce implementation burden.

## C09. Reducing Symbiosis Bias through Better A/B Tests of Recommendation Algorithms

**Read: F, latest arXiv PDF §§3-6 inspected.** [Certain] [Primary publication record](https://research.google/pubs/reducing-symbiosis-bias-through-better-ab-tests-of-recommendation-algorithms/), [paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.07107). The paper formalizes interference from shared training data and compares cluster randomization, data diversion, and user-corpus co-diversion. Simulation uses 100 users, 1,000 items, 100 periods, and oracle/random/item-CF/user-CF algorithms; industrial evidence analyzes a country-diverted exploration experiment. [Certain] §6 p.8 states that theory omits heterogeneity, simulations cover limited algorithms/parameters, and real pre-post evidence does not fully identify the cluster-quality/bias relationship. [Likely] A synthetic environment alone cannot validate an online correction against actual user feedback. **Extension, high computational but low empirical closure feasibility:** heterogeneous-user simulation comparing adaptive recommender updates and split designs with known TTE, plus sensitivity analysis. A three-day simulation can establish conditional mechanisms; it cannot replace the missing platform meta-experiment.

## C10. A LLM-based Controllable, Scalable, Human-Involved User Simulator Framework for Conversational Recommender Systems

**Read: F, arXiv v1.** [Certain] [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.08035v1), [author demo/code](https://github.com/zlxxlz1026/CSHI). CSHI uses profile/preference/message plugins, excludes the target item's name until recommendation, distinguishes known/unknown preferences, and coarsens sensitive target attributes. §3 evaluates ReDial/OpenDialKG movie dialogues and a MovieLens scenario without supplied dialogue using recall/success/turn metrics and leakage filters. [Certain] §3.1 explicitly acknowledges remaining leakage from original conversation histories. No dedicated limitations section was found in this version. [Likely] Better success under a simulator does not imply that simulator preserves human rankings of recommender policies; target-derived preferences can still make some policies artificially easy. **Extension, medium feasibility for simulation-only audit:** fixed hidden target with alternative valid preference disclosure policies, measuring reversals of pairwise recommender rankings at matched information budgets. Human ranking validation is necessary for a realism claim and is not guaranteed by a three-day compute budget.

## C11. Selective Multi-grained Alignment for Text-Video Retrieval

## C12. Uncertainty-Aware Fusion: An Ensemble Framework for Mitigating Hallucinations in Large Language Models

**Read: F.** [Certain] [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.05757v1), [WWW Companion DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3701716.3715523). UAF ranks models by validation accuracy times uncertainty-detection AUROC, then selects an instance response through accuracy and inverse uncertainty. It evaluates TruthfulQA, FACTOR-news, and TriviaQA with several open models and perplexity/Haloscope/semantic entropy; 10% is held out for selection and K tuning. [Certain] §4 admits inference cost linear in ensemble size and additional uncertainty overhead, proposing dynamic selection. [Likely] Within-dataset validation cannot establish calibration after domain/answer-style shift; raw confidence scores also need not be comparable between models. **Extension, high feasibility with cached predictions:** domain-held-out calibrated selection under fixed total generation budgets, comparing best-single, uniform ensemble, UAF, and simple learned routing. Generic uncertainty-aware ensembles or dynamic model selection are already prior work and cannot anchor a novelty claim alone.

## C13. Unseen Fake News Detection Through Casual Debiasing

**Read: F.** [Certain] **Casual** is the published arXiv title spelling; the mathematical narrative says **causal**. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.04160v1). FNDCD estimates an environment variable and reweights a GCN classification/propagation-structure objective. Twitter/Weibo open-domain data train models evaluated on opposite-language COVID19 news; accuracy and per-class F1 are reported. [Certain] §6 explicitly acknowledges the fixed prior ratio, simplified content-environment dependence, and need for real-time scalability. [Likely] The experiment simultaneously changes language, platform, time, and topic; improved prediction does not identify which shift was corrected, nor establish the inferred variable is a causal environment. **Extension, medium feasibility:** factorial language/topic/platform tests where feasible, source-only prior selection, and propagation truncation at fixed observation times. Compare simple confidence reweighting and structure regularization separately. Propagation-based classification does not automatically transfer to early detection before such interactions exist.

## C14. A Cross-Domain Study of the Use of Persuasion Techniques in Online Disinformation

**Read: F.** [Certain] [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.15098v1). A SemEval-trained RoBERTa persuasion classifier tags 16 sufficiently frequent techniques in CIDII, COVID19, Climate Fever, and EUvsDisinfo; non-English EU material is translated with GPT4o. Analyses compare technique odds ratios and LIWC associations across domains. [Certain] §2 excludes trustworthy articles and rare techniques; §3.2 limits the main-text contextual analysis to climate because of space. No dedicated limitations section was found. [Likely] Differences in predicted technique frequency can combine true prevalence with domain-specific classifier error, translation effects, and collection policy. Classifier outputs alone do not separate those components. **Extension, high compute/medium annotation feasibility:** stratified cross-domain manual calibration, error-adjusted prevalence intervals, and translated/original paired audits. The useful contribution would be a measurement correction with verified annotation, rather than merely applying another LLM classifier or adding a new domain.

## C15. JudgeBlender: Ensembling Automatic Relevance Judgments

**Read: F, arXiv version titled Ensembling Judgments for Automatic Relevance Assessment.** [Certain] [Final-title author PDF](https://bhaskar-mitra.github.io/files/3701716.3715536.pdf), [full preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.13268v1), [code](https://github.com/rahmanidashti/JudgeBlender). PromptBlender combines prompts; LLMBlender combines Mistral7B/Gemma7B/Llama3-8B judgments. LLMJudge/TREC-DL2023 supplies 25 development and25 test queries. Metrics cover label agreement, ranking correlation, and §5.4 ranking-system model-family bias. [Certain] §6 acknowledges one dataset, few panel/prompt settings, and open quality-cost optimization. [Likely] Strong overall system correlation can coexist with errors on close system decisions, minority queries, or particular relevance grades. **Extension, high computational feasibility:** decision-sensitive evaluation under fixed annotation budgets with frozen held-out systems/topics. **Novelty warning:** 2026 papers now directly study correlated judge errors and significance distortions; generic diversity, error-correlation, or cost-aware judge selection is not defensibly new.

## C16. Towards Safe Synthetic Image Generation On the Web: A Multimodal Robust NSFW Defense and Million Scale Dataset

**Read: F.** [Certain] [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.11707v1), [author repository](https://github.com/shahidmuneer/multimodal-nsfw-defense). A CLIP-based text/image cross-attention classifier is trained on a million prompt/image pairs from source datasets, SDXL generation, and adversarial variants. Evaluation reports content-classification metrics and attack success under I2P/MMA/QF/PGD conditions. [Certain] §4 says the generated dataset is **not publicly released**; §5 admits English-only data, cultural/context limits, and safe-domain dependence on COCO, suggesting matched safe examples and multilingual expansion. [Likely] Source/generator differences correlated with class can create shortcuts; a random split is insufficient to rule them out. **Extension, medium only if usable data/checkpoint exists:** held-out-generator and benign-context matched tests with text/image contradiction controls, reporting false positives. ## C17. Pushing The Limit of LLM Capacity for Text Classification

**Read: F, arXiv v2; WWW Companion record has shorter publication length than preprint.** [Certain] [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.07470v2), [publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3701716.3715528). RGPT iteratively fine-tunes boosted base learners on reweighted labeled examples, then feeds preceding predictions/error rates into subsequent learners. SST2/MR/AGNews/Ohsumed evaluate accuracy and macro-F1, with baseline, iteration, error, and human-comparison analyses. [Certain] §7 explicitly lists repeated fine-tuning expense, four-dataset scope, homogeneous base learners, and small-data overfitting risk. [Likely] A zero-shot prompt template does not make supervised task-specific fine-tuning zero-shot learning; comparisons must match training-data access and compute. **Extension, medium feasibility at small scale:** compare repeated boosting against equal-token continued fine-tuning, checkpoint averaging, and independent ensembles under domain/label-noise shifts. Simply switching to newer LLMs or heterogeneous learners is derivative and already foreshadowed.

## C18. RALLRec: Improving Retrieval Augmented Large Language Model Recommendation with Representation Learning

**Read: F.** [Certain] [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06101v1), [author code](https://github.com/JianXu95/RALLRec). It augments item descriptions with LLM knowledge, aligns textual and collaborative embeddings, retrieves relevant user history, and combines similarity with recency. Despite the recommendation title, §3's evaluated target is rating-derived **CTR prediction**, measured by AUC/log-loss/accuracy on BookCrossing, MovieLens1M, and Amazon Movies&TV. [Certain] Its reranker uses tuned channel weights and reciprocal positions; no dedicated limitations section was found. [Likely] Generated descriptions can introduce unsupported attributes, while binary prediction improvements do not establish full-catalog next-item ranking quality. **Extension, medium feasibility:** controlled description corruption and provenance filtering with chronological interaction-only embeddings, evaluating both classification and ranking under the same candidate contract. Compare title-only, verified metadata, generated descriptions, and popularity/recency baselines; do not assume an embedding-generated improvement is evidence-grounded.

## C19. Evaluating Personality Traits in Large Language Models: Insights from Psychological Questionnaires

**Read: F.** [Certain] [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.05248v1), [WWW Companion DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3701716.3715504). Five inventories are administered to five GPT/Llama models after GPT4o paraphrasing checked through embedding similarity. Questions are randomized in batches of10, with100 minimum-temperature iterations; means, variability, and trait dominance are compared. [Certain] §3.2 excludes TIPI from cross-inventory CV because its Likert scale differs; §4 calls for careful questionnaire design. No dedicated limitations section was found. [Likely] A paraphrase's semantic similarity cannot prove training-data absence or construct validity; repeated low-temperature self-reports cannot establish behavioral personality. **Extension, high compute/medium scientific feasibility:** preregister scale-normalized invariance and held-out behavioral prediction, with semantic-equivalent paraphrases and user-response-format controls. **Collision:** a 2025 Nature Machine Intelligence framework already evaluates LLM personality reliability/construct validity and downstream text behavior, so generic questionnaire-to-behavior validation is not new.

## C20. RAG-Enhanced Evidence Recommendation in Financial Legal Resolutions

**Read: A, institutional publication record only; full paper unavailable.** [Certain] [NYCU primary record](https://scholar.nycu.edu.tw/en/publications/rag-enhanced-evidence-recommendation-in-financial-legal-resolutio/), [WWW Companion DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3701716.3715520), pp.1096-1099. Specific split protocol, models, baselines, numerical performance, and code/data release were not verified. **Author limitations: unavailable.** [Likely] Because final judgments are present somewhere in the corpus, the input boundary and chronological retrieval cutoff require inspection before excluding outcome leakage; this is not an accusation of actual leakage. **Extension, low present feasibility:** chronology-aware evidence ranking and citation-entailment auditing with adjudication-time inputs. Domain expert labels and corpus access are prerequisite; a three-day API run cannot substitute for them.

## Three strongest conditional opportunities from this partition

1. **Compression that preserves retrieval decisions (C01).** [Likely] The most practical computational direction is to audit which queries lose relevant top-k items under configurable depth/width, then spend compute selectively based on calibrated rank instability. Establish a fixed candidate pool, untouched query/domain test, actual wall-time and memory measurement, and equal-cost baselines. A statistical guarantee about matching a full ranker must be separated from relevance correctness; the full ranker can also be wrong. **Medium feasibility**, contingent on accessible trained configurable checkpoints. Novelty is unconfirmed, and generic early exit is already occupied.

2. **Logical faithfulness of document-to-query generation (C05).** [Likely] Training-free source copying is computationally suitable, but token provenance is weaker than preserving relation, attribution, and polarity. Controlled source minimal pairs can isolate that gap with deterministic edit provenance, real retrieved passages, and human-checked generated-query entailment. A useful target is preventing query expansion from asserting a negated/attributed entity relation while preserving retrieval benefit. **Medium feasibility** with local small-model internals and a bounded annotation set. Generic negation sensitivity and hallucination diagnosis are already studied; a specific query-generation intervention needs further novelty screening.

3. **Anchor similarity versus actual modality faithfulness (C08).** [Likely] EARec's title-RougeL proxy provides a concrete falsifiable target: does high alignment similarity imply that item attributes actually cause the right recommendation changes? Freeze representations/checkpoints; intervene separately on text/image/behavior while controlling title and item identity, and compare with unimodal/unaligned/merged variants. Measure ranking effects and alignment independently; a correlation across a handful of model variants is insufficient. **Medium feasibility** using released weights, with artifact readiness unexecuted. This would need a distinct modality-intervention benchmark or mechanism, not just another explanation score.

[Certain] None of these three opportunities has been experimentally validated here or established as novel. They are narrower hypotheses with a tractable causal contrast. In this partition, generic ensemble judging and generic user-simulator validation have especially strong prior-art collisions. The root's alternative thematic-aggregation direction may therefore be a better place to spend the remaining novelty work.

## B01 — Contextualized Counterspeech: Strategies for Adaptation, Personalization, and Evaluation

**F. WWW 2025.** DOI [10.1145/3696410.3714507](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714507). Inspected [arXiv v3 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.07338v3), §§4-7.

[Certain] The study compares 36 LLaMA2-13B configurations combining counterspeech fine-tuning, political-community adaptation, conversation parents, and user history/summaries. Evaluation uses 128 toxic Reddit comments from five US-political communities; seven selected configurations and 20 representative responses per configuration enter a preregistered crowdsourcing study. Some lightweight contextual configurations improve perceived adequacy/persuasiveness; automatic rankings disagree substantially with human rankings. **Author limitation (§7):** one LLM, limited strategies/indicators, and crowdsourcing representativeness/reliability. **Reviewer boundary [Likely]:** observer ratings of likely persuasion do not identify reduced subsequent toxicity by the actual author. **Extension proposal:** test history relevance and provenance under corrupted or irrelevant context, with blinded human adequacy ratings and preregistered evaluation selection. **Feasibility: medium** for generation/robustness; low for a real intervention trial. Novelty must include the same authors' [2026 follow-up](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26236), which already re-examines contextualization and evaluation.

## B02 — Detecting Linguistic Bias in Government Documents Using Large Language Models

**F. WWW 2025.** DOI [10.1145/3696410.3714526](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714526). [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.13548), §§3-6. The source title capitalizes “language” differently; this is the same publication.

[Certain] DGDB contains 3,747 expert-annotated Dutch government sentences, collected through a seed list; four BERT-family configurations are compared with GPT-3.5/GPT-4o-mini prompting. Evaluation includes a 60/20/20 split and a separate unseen-rare-term regime; the latter excludes 12 terms/69 instances from training. BERTje's reported F1 falls from .812 to .554 across those regimes. **Author limitation (§6):** seed terms may omit bias forms, and transfer requires queryable government records and consensus on terms. **Reviewer boundary [Likely]:** held-out terms within a keyword-selected collection do not measure recall on arbitrary government sentences outside that collection mechanism. **Extension proposal:** a collection-shift audit with randomly sampled documents and explicit annotation uncertainty. **Feasibility: medium**, limited by Dutch annotation. Generic open-set, RAG, juror-agent, rewrite/verify extensions are already occupied by [MARS-Gov, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11136), not novel proposals by themselves.

## B03 — SPRec: Self-Play to Debias LLM-based Recommendation

**F. WWW 2025.** DOI [10.1145/3696410.3714524](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714524). [arXiv v3 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.09243v3), §§3-7; [official implementation](https://github.com/RegionCh/SPRec).

[Certain] SPRec iteratively uses the current recommender's outputs as negative examples, with SFT and DPO steps, to counter item concentration. Experiments cover MovieLens, Goodreads, Amazon CDs/Vinyl, and Steam, chronologically split 8:1:1; each method uses 4,096 training interactions, 512 validation interactions, and 1,000 test interactions. Metrics include HR/NDCG, distinct-item diversity, top-three over-recommendation, and category-distribution mismatch. **Author limitation (§7):** popularity bias inherited during SFT is not solved; sequential rather than isolated preference alignment remains open. **Reviewer boundary [Likely]:** matching historical category distributions is a declared fairness proxy, not proof of unbiased latent preferences or better long-term welfare. **Extension proposal:** evaluate off-policy concentration/utility over sequential recommendation rounds while separating historical preference from exposure. **Feasibility: medium** with released small samples; low for causal online welfare. A simulator's assumed response law must not be presented as human validation.

## B04 — Analyzing User Characteristics of Hate Speech Spreaders on Social Media

**F. WWW 2025.** DOI [10.1145/3696410.3714502](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714502); publication verified in [official proceedings](https://archives.iw3c2.org/www2025/www2025-proceedings.pdf). [Manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.15772), §§3-6.

[Certain] The method combines hate-topic clustering, inverse-propensity debiasing, matrix-factorized past latent vulnerability, and an explainable boosting machine. The augmented Founta Twitter dataset contains 25,196 root posts and 65,920 reshares; evaluation uses five repeated train/test runs and interpretable model baselines. Associations differ across racist/misogynistic versus political hate clusters. **Author-stated limitation:** no dedicated limitation section was located in the inspected manuscript; §5.5 explicitly reports that PLV variants' predictive improvement is not statistically significant. **Reviewer boundary [Likely]:** modeled propensity/latent vulnerability does not by itself guarantee that all unobserved exposure/confounding is removed. Thus changing verification badges or follower displays is not causally validated by these associations. **Extension proposal:** sensitivity bounds and out-of-time/user-held-out transport of subgroup effects before intervention design. **Feasibility: low-medium**, depending on access to the augmented resharing graph and current tweet availability.

## B05 — Evaluating Robustness of LLMs on Crisis-Related Microblogs across Events, Information Types, and Linguistic Features

**F. WWW 2025.** DOI [10.1145/3696410.3714511](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714511). [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.10413), §§3-5; [author-hosted PDF](https://www.ferdaofli.com/_files/ugd/aff495_379f8d3566c84868a751cc5c9a9b70d0.pdf).

[Certain] Six models classify HumAID test tweets across 19 disasters and nine retained information categories (15,160 tweets after excluding “other relevant”). The study varies zero/few-shot examples and analyzes event types, country-language setting, and linguistic features; GPT-4/4o are stronger than the tested open models, while requests/urgent-needs and floods remain difficult. **Author future work (§5):** causal reasons/actionable fixes for category-specific errors and multimodal extensions remain unexamined. **Reviewer boundary [Likely]:** country grouping is not a multilingual test, and aggregate F1 does not encode operational costs of missed requests. **Extension proposal:** event-held-out selective classification with critical-class false negatives reported at a fixed human-review budget. **Feasibility: high** for bounded inference. Generic selective adjudication is already tested by a [2026 primary study](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/16/11/5621); a novel claim needs a more specific nonexchangeable-event/risk-control contribution.

## B06 — Unmasking Gender Bias in Recommendation Systems and Enhancing Category-Aware Fairness

**F. WWW 2025.** DOI [10.1145/3696410.3714528](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714528). [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.17921), §§3-7.

[Certain] The paper defines category-aware exposure/rank metrics and penalizes between-gender category-distribution differences during recommendation training. Evaluation uses MovieLens 100K, MovieLens 1M, and Yelp; five base recommenders and two fairness-aware models are compared, with HR/NDCG@50 and a 70/10/20 user-based split. **Author scope limitation (§3.1.2):** only binary genders are represented. **Reviewer boundary [Likely]:** minimizing marginal category disparity is a normative demographic-parity target; it does not separate stereotype amplification from genuine conditional preference differences. Additional gender groups cannot be fabricated or inferred from names simply to create an intersectionality experiment.

## B07 — Modality Interactive Mixture-of-Experts for Fake News Detection

**F. WWW 2025.** DOI [10.1145/3696410.3714522](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714522). [arXiv v2 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.12431v2), §§3-4 and Appendix A.

[Certain] MIMoE-FND routes fusion through interaction experts based on unimodal prediction agreement and CLIP semantic alignment. It is evaluated on Weibo, Weibo-21, and GossipCop against multimodal baselines, with gating ablations and case studies. **Author observation (§4.1):** celebrity entertainment images in GossipCop only partly reflect the interaction diversity of user-generated Weibo posts; no dedicated limitations section was located. **Reviewer boundary [Certain protocol; Likely impact]:** Appendix A.2 reports the best test accuracy across 50 epochs at a single fixed seed. This introduces test-based model selection; the amount of optimism has not been measured here. **Extension proposal:** validation-selected repeated runs plus event/source-disjoint and missing-modality tests, isolating whether interaction gating generalizes. **Feasibility: medium**: the authors report approximately five hours per 50-epoch run on one A40; hardware/data access still need a preflight. A protocol correction alone is not automatically a novel main-track method.

## B08 — Cross-Modal Transfer from Memes to Videos: Addressing Data Scarcity in Hateful Video Detection

**F. WWW 2025.** DOI [10.1145/3696410.3714534](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714534). [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.15438), §§3-5; [official artifact](https://github.com/Social-AI-Studio/CrossModalTransferLearning).

[Certain] Human-assisted label alignment combines original meme labels, VLM predictions, and human conflict adjudication. FHM/MAMI memes substitute for or augment MHC/HateMM videos using LLaMA-3.2-11B and LLaVA-NeXT-Video-7B. The formulation explicitly uses text/vision, excluding audio (§3.1), and merges MHC hateful/offensive labels. **Author limitation (§5):** observed gains plateau as dataset size grows; larger-model benefits are proposed, not measured. **Reviewer boundary [Certain protocol; Likely impact]:** §4.1.3 explicitly selects the best epoch on video-test performance. This creates optimistic checkpoint-selection risk. **Extension proposal:** a preregistered validation-selected, creator/source-disjoint replication that separates label-definition transfer from modality transfer, including independent blind labels on disagreement cases. **Feasibility: medium** for a bounded experiment; video availability and reannotation cost are material. Existing results do not prove that audio inclusion will improve performance.

## Three strongest opportunities from this partition

These are **candidate directions, not certified novel ideas**. Their worth comes from explicit task/measurement boundaries, not the addition of another agent.

1. **When global consistency deletes valid evidence.** Start from B16/B18 and compare naive extraction, Graphusion, provenance-preserving multigraphs, and MemGraphRAG-style adjudication on compatible multirelations, genuinely contradictory claims, temporal changes, and contextual qualifiers. Formal target: preserve all source-entailed claims while deleting only claims contradicted under the same scope; measure false-conflict deletion and supported-claim recall separately from QA. Include a controlled corpus for identifiability and a blinded real-paper sample for external validity. A three-day inference experiment is plausible. **[Likely]** This is the strongest precise representational boundary here; “add provenance” is already occupied. **Novelty status: unresolved**, especially against HierarchRAG and other 2026 consistency systems.

2. **A conserved annotation budget for graph prompting.** Start from B14/B19. Define one set of revealed labels; charge every neighbor label, demonstration, development label and oracle call to it. Study allocating a label to a central neighbor versus a representative demonstration, allowing legal reuse of the same queried label. Compare random/degree/uncertainty/CoverICL/DSPy policies and a small GNN at identical budgets, under homophily shifts and training-only neighbor-label visibility. Small public TAG inference is feasible in three days. **[Likely]** The unresolved GraphiT label-access contract motivates the audit; it does not prove leakage. **Novelty status: unresolved** because LLM-GNN, CoverICL, and Locle already optimize annotation/coverage. The contribution must be joint budget accounting and allocation, not active selection alone.

3. **Do multimodal moderation improvements survive clean model selection and source transfer?** B07/B08 explicitly report test-driven selection. Freeze validation selection and thresholds, then evaluate source/creator/event-disjoint holdouts, taxonomy-aligned labels, repeated seeds and calibration. Separate gains from source augmentation, relabeling, and architecture. Efficient frozen-encoder baselines can fit the runtime budget; full VLM retraining across all conditions may not. **[Certain]** The selection protocols are present in the papers; **[Likely]** their reported estimates are optimistically selected, but the magnitude is unknown. **Novelty status: methodological audit**, not automatically a new algorithm; it becomes compelling only if a substantial, reproducible field-wide failure and an effective remedy are demonstrated.

## Newer-work exclusions and practical bounds

- [Certain] [MARS-Gov / The “10th Juror” (2026-10-08)](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11136), §§3-4, already combines legal RAG, open-set group screening, juror agents, conservative routing and rewrite verification on DGDB, including held-out terms/categories.

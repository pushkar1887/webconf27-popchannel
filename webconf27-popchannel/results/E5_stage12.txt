Scope: partial (Stage 1-2), not final Stage 3.
No new scoring/training, no Kaggle/network, no mappings recomputed.

Prediction copied from PR-E5:
For the GraphHash prior gains, >=60% of the added hits fall in the top-20%-popularity items; bottom-80% recall changes by <=0.5 points.

Operational choices (not retroactively preregistered):
Head = top ceil(20% of catalog) by train event count; ties ascending original item ID.
Added hits = gross newly recovered true-positive (user,item) pairs, before subtracting lost hits.
Tail primary = mean hits_tail/min(20,tail truth count), only users with tail truth.
Tail contribution = mean hits_tail/min(20,all truth count), over every evaluated user.
Activity quartiles = train event counts among evaluated users, ties original user ID.

Interpretation:
The head-added-hit prediction passes for every comparison. This does not imply tail safety: all three DirectAU comparisons fail the <=0.5pp tail prediction, even if tail is normalized as its contribution to overall recall.
Gowalla and Yelp DirectAU also show large declines in catalog coverage and tail recommendation share. Aggregate Recall@20 improvements must be reported together with this concentration.
Yelp BPR is marginally beyond the 0.5pp conditional-tail threshold (-0.525pp); its overall-denominator contribution is -0.254pp, so this borderline verdict depends on the preregistration denominator ambiguity.
AmazonBook BPR passes both operational conditions; this does not imply no tail decline (-0.472pp).
Every activity quartile has positive overall Recall@20 gain; that does not imply every user improves or that tail items improve.
AmazonBook uses one legacy training checkpoint and random tie key seed 0. Modern Gowalla/Yelp inputs use their saved torch.topk lists. Do not pool these as matched tie/optimization replications.
The JSON includes raw-baseline sensitivity for modern DirectAU, full popularity-quintile and activity-quartile tables, item coverage, gross losses and net changes.

Lineage checks passed for every baseline/corrected list (plus raw DirectAU sensitivities): original test user set, npos, per-rank or per-user hit field, NDCG, duplicate item exclusion and train+val masking. Modern scalar recall reproduced within 1e-7.
JSON numbers are fractions internally; multiply scores by 100 for percentages and deltas by 100 for percentage points.
